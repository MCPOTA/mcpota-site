Questions

Do I pay anything now?

No. The waitlist is free. You'll get a founding-member offer at launch and decide then.

What do you do with my email?

We use it only to send founding-member news and your launch offer. We never sell or share it, and you can ask us to remove it any time by emailing support@mcpota.com.

Is this an investment?

No. Founding membership is an early-access offer for the apps, not a share in the company.

Which phones?

Both apps are in testing on Android and iPhone now. Every MCPOTA app ships on both.
