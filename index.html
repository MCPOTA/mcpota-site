Our apps

Two apps. Same promise.

Every MCPOTA app is built for Android and iPhone, works on the phone first, and tells you what it will do before it does it.

Spygone

Know what's leaving your phone.

Checks for the signs of spyware, stalkerware and remote-access scams, and shows which apps use your location, contacts, texts and mic while you're not using them, who they talk to, and how much they send. Then fix it in a tap, and undo it in a tap.

  • Spyware check, including a public list of known stalkerware
  • A timeline of what each app actually did
  • A–F grades for each app's privacy policy
  • One-tap fixes, with a check that catches undone fixes
  • Shareable executive and technical reports

Startsy

The hardest part is starting. Startsy makes it small.

Say a task out loud. Startsy turns it into small steps that run all the way to done, and puts the first one right in front of you. Built for brains that freeze at the start, like many with ADHD.

"I've lived with ADHD my whole life. I built Startsy because I needed it: big tasks broken into small, easy ones. As my father-in-law told me, the journey of a thousand miles begins with one small step." — Nate Cain, founder
  • A clear finish line for every task
  • Today never holds more than three
  • Startsy the robot cheers you on, never scolds

About the founder

Nate Cain

Founder · MCPOTA

Nate has worked in cybersecurity since 1997 (nearly 30 years), protecting national security systems and critical infrastructure. He started in the U.S. Army running secure communications, then spent two decades on the front lines of federal cyber defense: hunting threats, assessing risk, and building the vulnerability-management programs that tell big organizations what's really happening on their networks.

Today he helps federal, state and local government agencies run those programs at scale. MCPOTA is the other half of that work: putting the same honest, evidence-first view into everyone's pocket, with apps that respect the person holding the phone.

MCPOTA is the software side of Cain & Associates LLC. When you need a person rather than an app, such as a forensic review of a phone you think is compromised, Cain & Associates is the services side.

  • U.S. Army — secure communications and encryption systems
  • U.S. Navy — information assurance in Yokosuka, Japan and at Pearl Harbor Naval Shipyard
  • Marine Corps Cyberspace Command — cyber protection team, defending critical infrastructure
  • FBI, VA, DEA and NAVSEA programs — lead security architect for enterprise vulnerability management
  • Cain & Associates — principal cybersecurity consultant: forensic device reviews, assessments and government work
  • MCPOTA — founder, building privacy-first apps
