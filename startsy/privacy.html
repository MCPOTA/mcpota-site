Startsy Privacy Policy

Startsy by MCPOTA · Android and iPhone · Effective October 17, 2026

In short

Startsy is a planner, not a privacy app. To help you, its optional AI needs to see what you're working on: the task's words and any photos you add. Here is exactly what goes where.

  • Your tasks are stored only on your phone. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Dictation uses your phone's own speech service. Startsy receives only the text.
  • Test builds can check for a newer version when you open the app, only if you allow it. No task data is sent.
  • "Break it down" is optional. It asks you first, then sends that one task's words and any photos you added to it to Startsy's AI server to get a finish line and steps back. MCPOTA doesn't store them; Anthropic, which runs the AI, deletes them within 30 days.

Information the app reads

  • What you type or dictate: each task's name, its "done when" line, its steps and which list it's in (Today, This week, Later, Done).
  • Speech recognition (only when you tap a microphone button): Startsy asks Android for microphone permission and opens a listening box that uses your phone's speech service to turn your voice into text. The mic stays on through pauses and stops when you tap Done or Cancel. On many phones that service is Google's, which may process the audio on its servers under Google's privacy policy. Startsy never keeps, stores or sends the audio; it keeps only the text. The first time, Startsy explains this and lets you type instead.

Startsy does not read your contacts, location, photos, files, calendar or other apps.

What the app can change on your phone

Startsy changes its own task list. When you give a task a start-by time, it also sets an alarm and shows a reminder notification on your phone at that time (with your permission to show notifications and, on Android 12 and later, to set alarms on time). It sets those alarms again after your phone restarts. You can turn all reminders off in Settings → Reminders, or remove a task's start time. Nothing else outside Startsy changes.

What is stored, and for how long

  • Your tasks are kept in Startsy's private storage on your phone until you delete them (Edit → Delete) or uninstall the app. Finished tasks stay in Done so you can undo.
  • A few settings are stored with them: whether you agreed to the dictation note, whether "Break it down" is on, whether the update check is on (with your tester access code, if you entered one), your Appearance choice (Match phone, Light or Dark), whether reminders are on, the name you gave Startsy and its notes about how you like to work, and which release notes you've already seen.
  • A task's start-by time is stored with the task. Reminders are made on your phone; nothing about them is sent anywhere.
  • Settings (⚙) → Delete all my tasks removes every task from your phone at once.
  • Startsy turns off Android's app backup, so your tasks are not copied to cloud backups.
  • MCPOTA keeps nothing about you on its servers. The AI server does not store or log the words it passes on.

When information leaves your phone

"Make this step smaller" (on the Start here card) works the same way but sends only that task's name and the one step.

When you tap "Break it down for me" and have agreed on the screen that explains it:

What is sentThe words of that one task (up to 300 characters), and up to 5 photos if you added them (for example your desk, the dishes or your computer screen). Photos are shrunk on your phone to about 1024 pixels and re-saved, which removes their hidden details such as location. Never your other tasks, your steps, your name or any account. The first time you include photos, Startsy reminds you to keep faces, cards, addresses, passwords and anything private out of the frame.
Where it goesMCPOTA's Startsy server, which runs on Cloudflare, passes the words and photos to Anthropic's Claude AI, which writes a finish line and steps. Cloudflare sees your internet (IP) address, as any website does, and uses it briefly to limit how many requests one network can make.
What is keptMCPOTA does not store or log the words or photos, and Startsy doesn't save the photos on your phone (they're dropped when you close the task form). Anthropic deletes API data within 30 days under its own policy and does not use it to train its AI (Anthropic's retention page).
How to stopSettings (⚙) → "AI breakdown is on" → tap to turn it off. Nothing is sent again unless you agree again. You can always type your own steps.

When you use Talk mode (talking with Startsy the robot) and have agreed on the screen that explains it:

What is sentWhat you say in Talk mode (turned into text by your phone's speech service), any photos you add there (shrunk and stripped of hidden details like location, up to 5 per conversation), the task you're talking about, and Startsy's short notes about how you like to work. Not your name: Startsy keeps it on your phone and sends a stand-in ("[name]") instead. Not your other tasks.
Where it goesThe same as Break it down: MCPOTA's Startsy server on Cloudflare passes it to Anthropic's Claude AI, which writes Startsy's reply and, when ready, the task's steps.
What is keptMCPOTA does not store or log conversations or photos. Anthropic deletes them within 30 days and does not train on them. On your phone, Startsy keeps your name and up to 30 short notes it learned (for example "has a clothes hamper"); it never notes health details, other people's names, addresses or money. See or delete them in Settings → Talking with Startsy. Startsy's voice is your phone's own text-to-speech.
GuardrailsStartsy sticks to starting and finishing tasks. It isn't a friend, therapist or medical service and doesn't treat anything. If someone mentions being in crisis, it points to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988 in the US) or 911.

Update check (test builds only). If you tap Allow on the "Check for updates?" screen and enter a tester access code, each time you open Startsy it asks MCPOTA's builds server whether a newer test build is posted:

What is sentYour tester access code and a request for Startsy's latest test build, and, if you tap "Update now", a download of it. No tasks, no name, no account.
Where it goesMCPOTA's builds server, which runs on Cloudflare, checks the code and fetches the build from MCPOTA's private GitHub repository. Cloudflare sees your internet (IP) address, as any website does, and uses it briefly to limit how many requests one network can make. GitHub does not see your phone.
What is keptMCPOTA's builds server stores and logs nothing. The access code is saved only in Startsy on your phone. The downloaded file is checked, handed to Android's installer (which asks you to confirm) and then deleted. No old builds are kept on your phone.
How to stopSettings (⚙) → "Update check is on" → tap to turn it off. The store versions of Startsy will not have this check; the store handles updates.

Things you send yourself. Two Settings items hand information to another app on your phone, and only when you tap them:

  • Export my tasks gives a text copy of your tasks to the app you pick (email, notes, files). From there it follows that app's rules.
  • Send feedback opens your email app addressed to support@mcpota.com, with the Startsy and Android version numbers filled in. You see the whole email and nothing is sent until you tap Send. MCPOTA uses it only to answer you and improve Startsy.

Nothing else leaves your phone. Startsy has no ads, analytics, crash reporting or tracking.

Children

Startsy is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, the effective date above changes and the app's release notes say what changed. Questions: privacy@mcpota.com.
