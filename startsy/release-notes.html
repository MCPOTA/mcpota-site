MCPOTA Startsy

Release notes

What changed in each version. The newest notes are also in the app under ⚙ → What's new.

v0.6.77 · October 28, 2026

  • 🔔 Nudge or ⏰ Alarm: when you set a start time, pick how Startsy reminds you, like on iPhone. A Nudge plays Startsy's tune once; an Alarm repeats it until you answer. New reminders start as a Nudge, and Startsy remembers your last pick.
  • Reminders stop after 3 swipes: swipe a reminder away and it still comes back in 10 minutes, but after 3 swipes in a row it stops ringing. The next time you open Startsy, it tells you and you can tap to pick a new time.
  • "Not today" → if you close the "When should Startsy remind you?" question without picking, Startsy no longer reminds you tomorrow anyway. It sets no reminder and shows "No reminder set. Tap to pick a time."
  • 📷 Snap a list now adds your tasks to Later instead of This week. Then you can pick up to 3 of them for This week, or tap Not now.
  • 📊 Spreadsheets: tap 📷 Snap a list, then 📊 Pick a spreadsheet, and pick an Excel (.xlsx) or CSV file with your tasks in it. Each row becomes a task to check, with its due date if the sheet has one. A column named Task (or To do, Subject or Title) works best; rows marked Done or Completed are skipped. Startsy reads the file on your phone, so nothing is sent anywhere and the file isn't kept. You see every task first: uncheck any you don't want, fix any words, then tap Add. Up to 100 at a time.
  • 📄 Attach a file now takes Excel (.xlsx) and CSV files too, as well as PDF and Word.
  • 📄 Attach a file: in Break it down and Talk mode, tap 📄 Attach a file to add a PDF or Word (.docx) file, like your resume, so Startsy knows more about the task. Startsy reads the text on your phone and shows it to you first, so you can delete anything private (address, phone, email). Only the text you keep is sent, after a screen that explains it, and the file itself never leaves your phone or is kept. Long files are cut to about 2 pages, and Startsy tells you. PDF text needs Android 15, or Android 11 to 14 with Google Play system updates; Word files work on every phone.
  • 📸 Take a photo is now one tap: Break it down and Talk mode have their own 📸 camera button next to 🖼 Choose photos, so you can take a picture right in Startsy. Camera photos stay out of your gallery.
  • Fixed: 🎤 dictation and Talk mode now work right after you use the camera, and the camera works right after you talk. Startsy lets go of the mic before the camera opens, and what you said so far is kept.
  • ✉ Send a report (Settings → Help): something not working? Startsy shows you its small app log first (what it did and any errors, with times; no tasks, words or photos), then opens an email to MCPOTA with it attached in your own email app. Nothing is sent until you tap Send. You can also clear the log there.
  • ＋ Add at the bottom now opens the Add a task form, so you can type a task right away (🎤 Speak is there too). To talk a task through with Startsy, tap 🎤 Talk at the top.
  • The privacy policy now also covers Startsy for iPhone (coming soon): Apple's speech recognition, Apple's photo picker, and its Nudge and Alarm reminders. Nothing changes in this Android app.

v0.4.48 · October 19, 2026

  • 📷 Snap a list: take a photo or pick a screenshot of any to-do list (Outlook or Planner on a work computer, a whiteboard, paper, sticky notes) and Startsy turns its lines into tasks. Tap 📷 Snap a list next to your list's name (or in the Add form or Talk mode). Startsy reads the picture on your phone, so the picture and its words aren't sent anywhere, and the picture isn't kept. You see every line first: uncheck anything you don't want, like a customer's name, fix any words, then tap Add. They go to This week, each with a first small step; use Break it down on any of them for more.

v0.3.46 · October 18, 2026

  • Fixed: Break it down and Make this step smaller now keep your name on your phone too (a stand-in is sent, as in Talk mode), and a damaged task file no longer empties your whole list.
  • 🔍 Make this step smaller: on the yellow Start here card, if a step feels too big, tap it and Startsy splits that one step into 2 to 4 smaller ones right where it was. Tap the banner to undo.
  • After Startsy saves a task in Talk mode, it asks when you'd like to start. Say "now" and it's ready in Start here, or say a time ("in an hour", "tonight", "tomorrow morning", "at 3") and it sets the start-by reminder. If it doesn't catch the time, a picker opens.
  • Talk mode flows better: when you finish taking photos (tap Done) or pick them from your gallery, Startsy looks at them right away and tells you what it sees. When it has a plan it asks "Want me to save this as a task?", and saying yes saves it. No extra button needed.
  • Softer sounds in Talk mode: a gentle two-note chime when Startsy starts listening and when it has heard you, in place of the phone's sharp beeps.
  • Tapping a task in your list now brings it up in the yellow Start here card instead of finishing a step. Steps are finished only from the yellow card, so nothing gets checked off by accident. The task in Start here has a ▶ in its circle. To see or change all its steps, tap ⋯ → Edit.
  • Taking photos flows better: after each photo Startsy asks "Photo 1 of 5 added" with big, easy-to-see buttons. Tap Take another to go straight back to the camera, or Done. Startsy uses up to 5 photos, so big jobs can show every corner.
  • Fixed: if you tell Startsy a different name ("actually, my name is Nate"), it now keeps the newest name, even after you close and reopen the app. You can also change it in Settings → Talking with Startsy.
  • 🎤 Talk mode: tap 🎤 Talk at the top (or 🎤 Add) and talk a task through with Startsy the robot. It says hi, learns your name, asks a few questions, asks for photos when that helps, and turns the conversation into a task with steps. Startsy talks back out loud and its mouth moves. Tap ☑ Tasks to go back.
  • Talk about any task: tap ⋯ on a task, then "Talk it through with Startsy".
  • Startsy remembers a few short notes about how you like to work (Settings → Talking with Startsy shows them, and you can delete any of them). Your name stays on your phone.
  • A cleaner task list: each task is one row. Edit, Start by, Back a step, Move and Let it go are under ⋯. Long-press 🎤 Add to type a task instead.
  • Startsy the robot has more room and more moves: when you finish a task it may walk across the top bar and back, do cartwheels, dance a jig, spin, hop or wave. Celebrations last a little longer so you can see them.
  • Startsy's motto greets you when you open the app: "A journey of 1,000 miles begins with one small step." It stays about 4 seconds so there's time to read it, or tap to skip.
  • 📷 Photos for Break it down: add up to 5 pictures of the job (your desk, the dishes, your computer screen) so the AI can see it and make the steps fit. Photos are shrunk first, their hidden details like location are removed, and they aren't saved. Startsy asks before sending them and reminds you to keep faces and private things out of the frame.
  • Meet Startsy the robot, up in the top bar. It's silent: a wave and a glowing antenna when you finish a step, and a different little trick when you finish a task. Settings → Startsy the robot can keep it still.
  • New app icon: Startsy the robot.
  • Clearer colors: the less important buttons are quieter so the next step stands out, and every color meets accessibility contrast standards.
  • Dictation is quieter and clearer: no more chimes while you talk. A moving bar shows Startsy is still listening, until you tap ✓ Done or Cancel.

v0.3.18 · October 17, 2026

  • The top bar stays put: Startsy's name, the version number and ⚙ Settings are always there while you scroll.
  • ⏰ Start by: give a task a time to start (in an hour, this evening, tomorrow morning, or any day and time). When it's time, Startsy plays its own short tune and keeps playing until you choose Start now, In 10 min or Not today. Not today asks when: tomorrow, in a week, in a month or a date you pick. Never "overdue".
  • Start now opens that task in Start here, at the step you're on.
  • 🗑 Let it go: drop a task you've decided not to do, in one tap. Undo appears for a few seconds.
  • Settings → Check for updates is always there, so you can check later if you were offline when Startsy opened. It says whether you have the newest build or couldn't reach the internet.
  • Settings → Reminders: turn all start-by reminders off or on, and change their sound or vibration in Android's settings.
  • New bottom bar: Today, Week, Later and Done (with how many tasks each holds) and a big 🎤 Add in the middle, all within thumb reach. Your list no longer scrolls behind anything.

v0.3.13 · October 17, 2026

  • ⚙ Settings: tap the gear at the top. It has Appearance (Match phone, Light or Dark), AI breakdown on/off, the update check, your data, release notes, feedback and About.
  • Your data: Export my tasks shares a copy as text to an app you pick, and Delete all my tasks clears this phone.
  • Send feedback opens your email app addressed to MCPOTA, with the Startsy and Android versions filled in. Nothing is sent until you tap Send.
  • What's new: after an update, Startsy shows what changed, once.
  • Dictation keeps listening while you pause or think. Tap Done when you've finished, or Cancel. Your words show as you talk.
  • The version number is shown at the top.
  • Light mode: the phone's status bar icons are now dark, so they're easy to see.
