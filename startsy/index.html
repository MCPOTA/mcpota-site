How it helps you start

🏁 A finish line

Every task says what done looks like, and its steps run all the way there, including the parts people forget, like sending it.

👣 Steps small enough to start

One step at a time. Stuck? Break it down, and later make a step smaller.

🎤 Voice first

Say the task. Pause and think; the mic keeps listening until you tap Done.

3️⃣ Today holds three

Today, This week and Later. Today never holds more than three tasks.

✨ Break it down (optional)

AI suggests a finish line and steps, only after you agree, and only that one task's words are sent.

🌗 Easy on the eyes

Light, dark or match your phone. No streaks, no points, no shame for missed tasks.

Private by design

Your tasks are stored only on your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics. You can export or delete them all from Settings. Read the privacy policy.

Startsy is a planner built on skills taught in proven programs. It isn't a treatment or medical advice.
