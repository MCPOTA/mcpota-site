Release notes

What changed in each version. The same notes are in the app under ⚙ → What's new.

v0.9.80 · October 13, 2026

  • New name: MCPOTA Privacy Watch. The home-screen icon still reads Privacy Watch.

v0.9.79 · October 13, 2026

  • Tabs renamed: Simple is now Summary, and Advanced is now Details, to match the executive summary and the detailed technical report (and to avoid confusion with "advanced access").

v0.9.78 · October 13, 2026

  • One-tap reports: "Email me this summary" at the bottom of Simple, and "Technical report" at the bottom of Advanced. The full 📄 Reports screen stays on the Dashboard.

v0.9.77 · October 13, 2026

  • ⚙ now opens a full Settings screen, grouped into Plan, Appearance, Protection, Help and Data.
  • Theme is one Auto / Light / Dark switch.
  • Reports moved to a 📄 Reports button on the Dashboard.

v0.9.75 · October 13, 2026

  • About screen in ⚙: version, plan, links and open-source licences.
  • What's new (this page) in ⚙.
  • Logs now record each update, to help with troubleshooting.
  • Fix check now covers fixes made before v0.9.72.

v0.9.72 · October 13, 2026

  • Fix check: Privacy Watch notices when an app update, reinstall or system update undoes a fix, and offers Re-apply or Leave it.
  • Trial: 7 days of full monitoring with one free one-tap fix. Fix it, reports and 7-day history are part of the full version.

v0.9.65 · October 13, 2026

  • Findings now show only what apps actually did: data sent, background access and trackers.
  • New 🛡 Settings audit, kept separate: screen readers, notification readers, device admins, keyboards, overlays, contactless-card apps, certificates, hidden apps and screen recording.
  • "How it could be misused": for every finding, what a company and what an attacker could do with it.
  • Report a false alarm: hide a finding and optionally send the details to support. Hidden findings can be shown again from ⚙.
  • Music and video apps scanning for speakers are amber, not red. Social uploads turn red only above 5 MB.

v0.9.50 · October 12, 2026

  • New top bar on every screen: Simple, Advanced, Dashboard and ⚙.
  • Settings menu with Dark/Light mode, Reports, Banking mode and Logs.
  • PDF reports: faster, smaller, with a progress window while they build.
  • Executive and technical reports by email, or downloaded.
