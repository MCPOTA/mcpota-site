Website privacy

We count clicks, not people.

This page covers the mcpota.com website only. Our apps have their own privacy policies: Spygone and Startsy. The apps themselves have no analytics.

What we count

To see which pages help and which don't, this site adds up, per page and per day:

  • how many times the page was opened;
  • which links and buttons were clicked (the words on the button and where the link goes);
  • how far down the page people scrolled (25%, 50%, 75% or all the way);
  • the name of the site that sent people here, such as "google" or "substack" (never the full web address);
  • whether the screen was phone-sized or computer-sized.

What we don't collect

No cookies and nothing stored on your device. No IP addresses, no browser or device details, no visitor or session IDs, nothing you type. Each visit only adds 1 to a daily total, so two visits can't be connected to each other or to you. We don't use Google Analytics, ad pixels or session recording, and nothing is shared with or sold to anyone.

How it works

A small script on our pages sends those counts to our own counter, a program we run on Cloudflare (a web hosting company). To slow down abuse, the counter looks at the sending IP address for a moment and then forgets it; it is never saved. Daily totals are kept for up to 13 months.

Saying no

If your browser has "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control turned on, or blocks scripts, nothing is counted at all.
