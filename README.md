# mcpota.com

Static site for MCPOTA, served by GitHub Pages at mcpota.com.

- `index.html`: MCPOTA company home page (what MCPOTA stands for, the apps, founder, founding members)
- `privacy-watch/`: Privacy Watch product page (hand-written)
- `founding-members/`: founding-member waitlist page (hand-written; the waitlist is an email draft, nothing stored on the site)
- `privacy-watch/privacy.html`: Privacy Watch privacy policy (mcpota.com/privacy-watch/privacy), copied from `docs/privacy.html` in the app repo
- `changelog.html`: Privacy Watch release notes (mcpota.com/changelog), built from the app's `whats_new.txt`; only its header and footer are hand-editable
- `startsy/`: Startsy pages (index is hand-written; privacy and release notes are published by the startsy repo's build)
- `samples/`: sample executive and technical reports (made-up apps and data)
- `img/`: phone screenshots used on the home page
- `img/team/`: founder photo
- `img/startsy/`: Startsy the robot (SVG, drawn from the app's RobotView)
- `img/brand/`: MCPOTA emblem, favicons, social image, and the emblem video
- `favicon.ico`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `site.webmanifest`: browser icons
- `CNAME`: custom domain
