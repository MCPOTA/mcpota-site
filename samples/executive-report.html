ChatLoop

🔴 🔏 Privacy risk

What it did: Sends your data in the background. Talks to advertising/tracking companies.

  • ⬆ Uploaded 14.2 MB while you weren't using it, to 31 different servers.
  • 🎯 Contacted 6 advertising/analytics services while closed (ads.sample-tracker.example, metrics.sample-analytics.example…).
Why it matters: Large uploads while you aren't using the app mean your data is leaving the phone without you asking for anything.
How it could be misused
  • 🏢 A company could send usage, device and personal data to its servers in the background without telling you.
  • 🥷 An attacker could quietly copy data off the phone — files, logs, recordings — while you're not looking.

Recommended: Tap Fix it, then: Battery → Restricted, and Mobile data → turn off Allow background data

Daily Weather+

🔴 🔏 Privacy risk

What it did: Tracks your location in the background. Talks to advertising/tracking companies.

  • 📍 Tracked your location 46× while you weren't using it (last 6:12 AM).
  • 🎯 Contacted 4 advertising/analytics services while closed.
Why it matters: Background location builds a detailed history of where you live, work and go — information advertisers and data brokers pay for.
How it could be misused
  • 🏢 A company could build a minute-by-minute history of where you live, work, shop, worship and see a doctor, and sell it to data brokers.
  • 🥷 An attacker could follow you in real time, learn your routine and know when your home is empty.

Recommended: Tap Fix it, then: Location → Only while using the app

FlashBright

🔴 🔏 Privacy risk

What it did: Read text you copied. Reads your contacts.

  • 📋 Read your clipboard 12× while closed (last 9:41 PM).
  • 👥 Read your contacts 3× in the background.
Why it matters: Copied text often contains passwords, one-time codes, addresses and messages. An app reading it in the background can quietly collect them.
How it could be misused
  • 🏢 A company could log what you copy — links, addresses, product names — to profile your interests and shopping.
  • 🥷 An attacker could harvest passwords, one-time codes and crypto-wallet addresses as you copy them.

Recommended: Tap Fix it, then: block clipboard reading and contacts access
